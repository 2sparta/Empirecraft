# Sound credits

The cassette cues use recordings of real tape mechanisms, trimmed and filtered for the mod.

| File | Recording | Author | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `cassette.ogg` | [U-matic VTR tape insert sound 2](https://freesound.org/people/LukaCafuka/sounds/843021/) | LukaCafuka | CC0 |
| `respawn.ogg` | [VHS Tape Insert and Eject sound](https://freesound.org/people/VCRman2014/sounds/788770/) | VCRman2014 | CC0 |

[CC0 terms](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The recordings remain CC0 and may be reused in forks.

`tools/prepare_cues.py` reproduces the edits using the high-quality MP3 previews from these pages. It converts to mono, cuts the unused parts, reduces room rumble and exports OGG.

`heartbeat.ogg` and `breathing.ogg` come from the original Epic Death Screen release. `tape.ogg` is generated by `tools/generate_tape.py`. These three assets and the code use the repository's MIT license.
