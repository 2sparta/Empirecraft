# Empirecraft death cues

The sixteen added OGG cues are original procedural recordings generated by
`tools/generate_audio.py` from deterministic sine waves, filtered noise and
envelopes. They contain no sampled third-party recordings, speech or music.
They are distributed under the MIT license in `LICENSE-upstream.txt`.

Mechanical grinding, a train approaching, cannon impact, a musket report,
fragment blast, steel ringing, spikes, boiling steam, electrical crackle,
a slowing pulse, dry wind, deep water, volcanic rumble, an experience drain,
wood/stone impact and an arrow flight are short one-shot cues. They use the
existing cassette-volume setting, are quieter than full scale, and stop when
the screen is skipped, removed, finished or interrupted by revival.

The original five recordings and their credits are unchanged; see
`AUDIO-CREDITS-upstream.md` and the original `AUDIO_CREDITS.md` inside the JAR.
